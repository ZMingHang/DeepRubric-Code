01 Introduction

Overview

DeepRubric generates research queries and evaluation rubrics from evidence retrieved from Wikipedia and OpenScholar.

An evidence tree organizes retrieved evidence into sub-questions and atomic evaluation targets.

The selected leaves provide the basis for factual and logical rubric criteria. The research query and its rubric are then generated together.

Read the full paper
Research summary

DeepRubric builds query-rubric supervision from retrieved evidence before reinforcement learning. Instead of starting from a user query and asking a model to invent a rubric, DeepRubric first constructs evidence trees from local Wikipedia and OpenScholar corpora, then synthesizes aligned research queries and grounded factual/logical rubric criteria from selected evidence leaves.

The release exposes the full reproduction loop: deploy retrievers, construct evidence trees, verify KEEP/REVISE/DROP samples, convert verified data to verl-tool parquet format, and start GRPO training for a tool-using deep research agent.

02 Method

Method Overview

The pipeline consists of evidence-tree construction, query-rubric synthesis, and GRPO training.

  1. 01 / CONSTRUCT

    Build evidence trees

    Expand a seed topic into sub-questions and retrieve supporting evidence for the atomic leaves.

  2. 02 / SYNTHESIZE

    Align queries and rubrics

    Aggregate from the leaves upward, then verify, revise, or drop the generated supervision.

  3. 03 / OPTIMIZE

    Train with rubric rewards

    Use the grounded criteria as GRPO rewards for a tool-using deep research agent.
